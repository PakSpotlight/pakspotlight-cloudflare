Installing it

Four steps, about a minute on a normal connection.

  1. Download the file

    Tap the download button above. The APK lands in your Downloads folder.

  2. Allow this one install

    Android will ask whether to allow installs from your browser. Tap Settings, then allow it. This is normal for any app that does not come from the Play Store.

  3. Install and open

    Open the downloaded file, tap Install, then open Classic TV Pakistan from your home screen.

  4. Start watching

    Pick something from the home rows or search the archive. Your place is remembered on this phone.

What is inside

The same archive the website serves, arranged for one hand: a hero pick, the rows you expect, a searchable grid, and a library that holds what you saved and what you watched — even with no connection.

Questions people ask

Why not the Play Store?

Play Store requirements now exclude Android 6 devices, and a large part of this archive's audience watches on exactly those phones. Distributing the APK directly keeps the app usable on them.

Will it work on my old phone?

The app itself supports Android 6.0 and newer. Episodes play through the YouTube player; if a particular video refuses to play on an older device, the Watch on YouTube button opens it in the YouTube app instead.

Do I need an account, or will I be tracked?

No account, no login. My List and watch history are stored only on your phone. The app sends one anonymous ping — a random install id and the version number — so we can tell how many people are using which build. That is all.
